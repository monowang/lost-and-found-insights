# TORONTO LOST & FOUND INSIGHTS: KEYS, WALLETS, AND HIGH-RISE CAT ESCAPES

### 1. The TTC Commuter Tax: Keys & Wallets (24.97% vs 6.60% Rest of Canada)

* **The Data:** Misplaced keys and wallets account for **24.97%** of all lost items in Toronto. In the rest of Canada outside Toronto, commuter belongings account for only **6.60%** of reports.
* **Why the huge difference?** Unlike car-centric Canadian cities, Toronto’s reliance on public transit (TTC subway lines, streetcars, Union Station GO connections) creates high-friction movement. Pocket items frequently slip out during rush-hour commutes or while switching transit lines.
* **Top Commuter Loss Hubs:** Union Station, TTC subway stations, and Bloor St West corridors.

---

### 2. The Condo Cat Phenomenon: Cats Out-Disappear Dogs

* **The Data:** Unlike suburban regions where lost dogs dominate shelter flyers, Toronto sees **cats (55.84% of lost pets)** reported missing significantly more often than dogs. 
* **Coat & Breed Breakdown:**
  * 🐱 **Cats:** **Tabby** (Brown/Black/Grey), Tuxedo, and Siamese lead missing reports. Peak disappearance hour is **6:00 PM (Evening)**.
  * 🐶 **Dogs:** **Labrador Mixes**, Poodle/Maltese Mixes, and Shepherds lead missing reports. Peak disappearance time is **Afternoon (12:00 PM – 5:59 PM)**, coinciding with park outings.
* **The ID Challenge:** Over **69%** of lost pets in Toronto were reported **without a collar or tag**. High-rise condo living leads to "indoor cat syndrome"—cats slip out open balcony doors or hallway doors uncollared, making digital neighborhood cross-posting the primary recovery mechanism.

---

### 3. Theft & Robbery Ratio: Nearly Double National Averages

* **The Data:** Reports explicitly indicating that the owner suspects item/pet theft or robbery stand at **4.65% in Toronto**, compared to **2.38%** across the rest of Canada.
* **Hotspots:** Concentrated around nightlife corridors (King West, Queen West) and high-volume summer event venues (RBC Amphitheatre / Budweiser Stage waterfront).

---

### 🏆 Toronto Recovery Highlights & Trends

* **Top Recovered Categories:** Keys (#1), Cats (#2), and Smartphones (#3).
* **Does Offering a Reward Boost Recovery?** Interestingly, **No**. Recovery rates for pets/items offering rewards ($250 median) were statistically identical to reports offering no monetary reward. Torontonians help return pets and items out of community care rather than financial incentive.
